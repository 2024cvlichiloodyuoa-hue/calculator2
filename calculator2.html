Methodology, equations and programming logic
  1. Target mean strength: f′ck = max(fck + 1.65 s, fck + X); s and X from the IS 10262:2019 assumed standard deviation table.
  2. w/c: relationship f′ck = K·(0.5/(w/c))^1.5 fitted to the IS 10262:2019 Fig. 1 curves per cement type (approximation; check against the printed chart), limited by IS 456 Table 5 maximum w/c for the exposure.
  3. Water: IS 10262:2019 water-content table (50 mm slump, angular aggregate), +3% per 25 mm extra slump, −10 / −20 kg/m³ for sub-angular / rounded aggregate, reduced by the admixture water reduction %.
  4. Cementitious = water ÷ (w/c), not less than IS 456 Table 5 minimum, flagged above 450 kg/m³ (IS 456 cl. 8.2.4.2).
  5. Coarse aggregate volume fraction: IS 10262:2019 table by nominal size and IS 383 Table 9 zone of fine aggregate, ±0.01 per 0.05 change of w/c from 0.5, ×0.9 for pumpable concrete.
  6. Absolute volume: V(agg) = 1 − (V cement + V SCM + V water + V admixture) [− air if selected]. CA = V·fraction·SG·1000; FA = V·(1 − fraction)·SG·1000.
  7. Moisture correction: dry mass = SSD/(1+abs); field mass = dry·(1+moisture); field water = water − Σ dry·(moisture − abs).
  8. Iteration: new w/c = w/c·(measured/target)^(1/1.5); water changed 3% per 25 mm slump shortfall or excess; cement = water/w/c. Ec and Fcr are compared with IS 456 (5000√fck, 0.7√fck).
  9. Table numbers marked as descriptions should be verified against your copy of the codes. Predicted values are never shown as verified; only trials with entered laboratory results count as verified.
